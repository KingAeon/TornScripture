## V2 — supported and unsupported mechanic boundary

The v0.1 registry admits base Xanax/Ecstasy/eDVD/Candy effects and the separately verified numeric gym-gain perk patterns. A complete verified perk/effect state with no material maximum-booster modifier may derive the base 86,400s booster threshold. Dynamic Candy/eDVD/drug effects, consumable cooldown changes, positive booster-maximum modifiers, active or unknown World Diabetes Day state, and unknown material gym notes fail closed for the affected prediction. The adapter retains raw planning inventory but omits unsupported preparation classes from planner-facing mechanics. It must never invent future drug cooldowns, precise event effects, or a stronger calibration label for an open-ended recommendation search.

Official [Torn OpenAPI](https://www.torn.com/swagger/openapi.json) still reports **6.13.6** at this freeze check. The documented paths remain `/user/bars`, `/user/cooldowns`, `/user/battlestats`, `/user/gym`, `/torn/gyms`, `/user/perks`, `/user/refills`, and `/user/inventory`. The `/user/gym` and `/user/perks` description/key-parameter disagreement remains capability-gated rather than guessed. The gym catalog remains Unstable and must be shape-guarded. Inventory is cached one hour per category and is useful for planning; it cannot alone prove LIVE inventory for execution. Energy, Happy, relevant cooldowns, gym, material gain modifiers, and required inventory keep the frozen execution-readiness freshness gates.

`/user/refills.energy` retains the used-state boolean polarity; `special_count > 0` blocks a ready paid-refill path until special-refill sequencing is modeled. The current paid Energy refill cost is a separately configured **30 Points**, verified against the [Torn Points reference](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Point), with no invented cash valuation. A separate current balance proof is needed for `pointsAvailable`; `/user/refills` alone cannot establish readiness or sufficient Points. Elevated-Happy confirmation that `bars.happy.maximum` remains the ordinary/base reference is still useful future strengthening; the adapter must never replace an unavailable ordinary Happy reference with elevated current Happy. Without an approved safe quarter-hour timing proof, elevated-Happy execution stays `NEEDS_REFRESH`. Unknown or stale source state suppresses only the dependent capability.
