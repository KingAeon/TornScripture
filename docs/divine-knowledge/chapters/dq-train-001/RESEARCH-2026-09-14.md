Checked: 2026-09-14. Status: source-audited; not live-player calibrated.

## Corrections to the initial discussion

1. The wiki equation is an estimated public model, not Torn's disclosed server code. It must not be presented as exact.
2. A later model exists. The blanket claim that Happy always has diminishing marginal returns is withdrawn: it was a property of the earlier equation, not a universal mechanic.
3. Above-50m growth requires separate treatment. Neither unrestricted linear use of the old equation nor a permanent 50m clamp is an acceptable current endgame model.
4. API availability does not prove freshness or effective Custom-key permissions.
5. Xanax stacking still has opportunity costs. Extra drug energy does not prevent natural regeneration being lost at/above the natural cap.
6. Source publication dates and present verification dates must remain distinct.

## Source ledger

### GYM — official wiki, public estimated model

[Gym](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Gym), checked 2026-09-14.

The listed constants match the preliminary chapter. Energy is per individual train; API gym gains use ten times the familiar dot scale. The page documents Happy consumption and the 2022 change to reduced-rate growth above 50m. It does not establish an exact contemporary endgame formula.

Disposition: retain as a named comparison model, not sole implementation truth. Model applicability and gym-unit normalization need explicit validation. Do not infer actual multi-train output by multiplying a frozen initial-state gain.

### VLADAR-V2 — primary community investigator, not official server formula

[Training Formula V2.0](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?a=0&b=0&f=61&p=threads&t=16182535), original 2020, edited 2022, checked 2026-09-14.

This later fit includes nested four-decimal rounding, an H^1.05 term, stat-specific offsets and random variation. Its author identifies the high-Happy correction as the least certain component. The displayed equation retains a 50m clamp despite a stat-cap-change header: internal staleness must be resolved.

It reports iterative batch trains, with updated stats and Happy, and Happy loss from rounding E/10 times a random integer 4–6. Its proposed average loss is 8/3 for a 5E train and 38/3 for 25E, not exactly E/2. Treat these as testable community findings.

Disposition: candidate model for calibration; not proof of current server behavior. Retrieve exact equation and coefficients from this primary source when implementing rather than duplicating another authoritative-looking formula here.

### HAPPY — official wiki mechanics

[Happy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Happy), checked 2026-09-14.

Over-base Happy resets on quarter-hour boundaries; temporary ceiling 99,999. Natural recovery is five per quarter-hour to base maximum. Staffed PI reference maximum is 5,025. Fitness Center's relevant special reduces gym Happy loss. Effects/books that preserve Happy must be separately identified; default reset assumptions cannot silently cover exceptions.

### CANDY — official wiki, rounding ambiguity retained

[Candy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Candy), checked 2026-09-14.

Base candy cooldown is 30 minutes. Documented example: 25 with a 1.5 faction multiplier gives 38. Book doubles and event triples effects; the combined table gives 225 for 25-base candy with faction/book/event. Grocery and Restaurant specials and a cooldown book affect relevant consumables.

Inference: this does not prove rounding after every modifier. Rounding 37.5 to 38 before multiplying by six gives 228, whereas the table gives 225. Test final-only versus staged rounding; do not encode universal ceiling behavior from one .5 example. Prefer item IDs over similar names. Prices on this page are references, not executable quotes.

### CD — official wiki, pre-use threshold

[Item Cooldowns](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Booster_Cooldown), checked 2026-09-14.

Booster cooldown normally limits further use once it reaches the player's maximum; capacity starts at 24 hours and faction upgrades can extend it to 48. Timer declines in real time. Consumable cooldown reductions must not automatically be applied to every booster.

Derived candidate: if current cooldown is c, limit L and item duration d, immediate slots are zero when c >= L, otherwise ceil((L-c)/d), assuming the documented pre-use threshold. Mixed items require checking every step. At c=0, L=86400, d=1800: 48 immediate items. Consume one, let one second pass: 48 additional slots are possible. Thus 49-candy recipes depend on elapsed time, not a universal constant. Boundary/latency behavior still needs live verification.

### DRUGS AND BOOSTERS — official wiki

[Ecstasy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Ecstasy): successful use doubles Happy; drug cooldown 200–231 minutes. Overdose removes Energy and Happy.
[Xanax](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Xanax): +250 Energy, +75 Happy, drug cooldown 360–480 minutes; overdose is a separate adverse outcome.
[Erotic DVD](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Erotic_DVD): +2,500 Happy, six-hour booster cooldown.
All checked 2026-09-14. No overdose probabilities were established in this pass. Never label a success-path forecast as risk-adjusted expected gain.

### ENERGY — official wiki

[Energy](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/Energy), checked 2026-09-14.

Baseline regeneration: five per ten minutes to 150 for donators; five per fifteen minutes to 100 otherwise. Maximum stacked Energy is 1,000. A daily points refill fills to natural maximum, rather than adding a full bar unconditionally.

Derived example: 24 hours continuously capped forfeits up to 720 ordinary donator Energy. Starting at 150 and adding four successful Xanax nominally supplies 1,150 but caps at 1,000. A fair simulator must count discarded energy, drug waiting time, refill timing, and ordinary training with the same resources.

## Next bounded step: calibration and specification

Proposed pure engine contract, not frozen:
- Inputs: model/version, stat identity/value, Happy/base/cap, normalized gym dots, energy-per-train, explicit supported modifiers, time/cooldowns, inventory quantities, timestamped price assumptions, budget and availability.
- Outputs: projected gain, ending state, resources spent/lost, assumptions, warnings and model status.
- Separate success-only scenarios from probability-weighted outcomes; unknown probabilities stay unknown.
- Candidate timelines: ordinary training, candy+Ecstasy, one-Xanax, full-stack/DVD, micros. Same horizon and comparable resources. Preserve leftover assets in comparisons.
- Owned items have zero new cash outlay but nonzero economic value; report both.
- Include a no-jump option. Abstain when uncertainty changes the ranking.
- Enforce gym eligibility, per-train energy granularity, reset timing and unsupported-modifier warnings.
- Long-horizon estimates must stop or downgrade when leaving the calibrated stat range; no unconditional 90-day precision.

Calibration request for the next step: use routine planned training, not extra spending. Locally record stat identity and before/after values, gym, starting/ending Happy, energy, train count, relevant perks, timestamp and displayed result. Compare single and batch events away from reset boundaries; cover low-Happy and normal-Happy cases before expensive jumps. Keep private observations local; only synthetic fixtures and aggregate nonidentifying error summaries belong here.

Acceptance gates: distinguish arithmetic correctness from live calibration; independently reproduce fixtures; investigate systematic residuals by stat/Happy/gym; choose tolerance after observing data, not to make a model pass. If high-Happy or >50m coverage is absent, explicitly mark unsupported. No implementation authorization, UI, network integration, timers, storage changes or release in this checkpoint.
