# Market Intelligence Domain

DQ-MARKET-001 established official current-source semantics. DQ-EXT-001 now establishes external/trader semantics.

Open roadmap anchors:

- issue #85 — Market Pulse: bottoming/reversal alerts;
- issue #108 — Event Outlook, Inventory Equity, Value at Risk.

Current Item Market order-book data is present-market context, not historical trend truth. Historical graph/source behavior requires separate validation before predictive or reversal language.

Market intelligence remains advisory and must not mutate Black Ledger accounting truth or automate Torn actions.
