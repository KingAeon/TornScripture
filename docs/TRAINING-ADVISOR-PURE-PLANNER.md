# Training Advisor pure planner checkpoint

`src/training-advisor-pure.js` implements the DQ-TRAIN-001A math kernel and the first DQ-TRAIN-001C pure planner slice. It exports CommonJS functions for the repository's Node tests. It is not loaded by a userscript. No browser, Torn API, market request, storage, clock, or gameplay action is present.

## Inputs and entry points

- `simulateTraining(request)` accepts the exact frozen 001A model request. Noise and Happy-loss rolls must be supplied. Its model descriptor retains the frozen arithmetic fixture label `candidate_not_live_calibrated`; planner confidence independently records the later bounded B1–B4 calibration status.
- `simulatePlanTraining({modelId, stat, happy, gym, gainPerks, energy})` provides a deterministic central planning path with explicit zero gain noise and loss roll 5. It stops at the first train that would *start* beyond 50m, preserving the modeled prefix and unspent Energy. This central path is not an EV or confidence interval.
- `generateEnergyCandidates(normalizedState)` enumerates train now, explicit cooldown Xanax checkpoints, natural full-bar wait, and a direct Point refill only below the natural Energy maximum. A refill sets Energy to `naturalEnergyMax` and reports `energyObtained = naturalEnergyMax - energyBefore`; it never adds Energy to a stack. It advances natural Energy through each wait from explicit `naturalRegen` ticks, preserves only capped lost regeneration, reports cap discard, and carries the post-Xanax drug cooldown. When Energy is below the natural cap and regeneration timing is missing, it withholds the delayed Xanax candidate. When Energy is already above the natural cap, a delayed candidate retains unknown lost regeneration; Balanced cannot score that unknown burden.
- `generateHappyFrontier({boosterSlots, items, newCashBudget})` and `generateHappyRecipes(state, itemMechanics, marketSnapshot, preferences)` enumerate owned/bought bundles in stable item-ID order, prune dominated recipes, and preserve cash and replacement value separately. Item effects and purchasable quantities must be explicitly normalized.
- `composePlan(...)` combines Energy and Happy preparation with sequential training, advisory actions, structural identity, economics, confidence, readiness, and a final-booster marginal comparison. With `postTrainRefill: true`, an explicitly allowed stacked plan trains the first Energy segment, uses one Point refill after Energy drops below natural max, verifies state, then trains the ordinary natural bar from the first segment's modeled final stat and Happy. This bounded flag is only generated for stacks above the natural max; no pre-stack Xanax/refill combination is generated. Both TRAIN actions remain advisory.
- `rankCandidates(...)` accepts normalized outcome summaries and implements all six frozen objectives and their confidence, useful-session, Pareto, and tie rules. `recommend(...)` composes the stages into the Recommendation contract.
- `resolveObserved`, `replanOnEvent`, and `compareRecommendationIdentity` are pure transition helpers. A caller must normalize fresh observed state and invoke replanning; no background observation exists here.

Normalized state must supply actual numeric stat, Happy, Energy, gym dots and Energy per internal train, supported explicit gain perks, and the chosen stat. `ordinaryHappy` is the explicit base/ordinary Happy for the natural full Energy bar usefulness reference; an elevated `happy` is never its fallback. Best Value, Use My Inventory, and Fastest Useful abstain when the ordinary reference is unavailable. Optional cooldowns, inventory, prices, regeneration, and refill mechanics enable their corresponding candidates only. An `activeEffects` entry that affects gym gain must be explicitly supported *and* linked to a matching `gainPerks` ID. Set `calibratedDomain: true` only after an adapter has verified the documented B1–B4 evidence domain; otherwise the planner uses `SUPPORTED_EXTRAPOLATION` and the selected risk policy decides eligibility.

For a delayed plan, `happyAtCheckpoint` must be supplied as an explicit normalized future checkpoint value before any full gain forecast is ranked. The plan includes `VERIFY_STATE` after waits, including the new post-Xanax drug checkpoint before Ecstasy; a fresh observation overrides that projection. Booster legality uses `boosterMaxSeconds - boosterSeconds` across generation, composition, and readiness. Immediate boosted plans require a safe quarter-hour window for `READY` status. The planner may rank a `NEEDS_ITEMS`, `NEEDS_REFRESH`, `WAITING`, or `BLOCKED` plan while keeping execution readiness separate.

Execution readiness requires explicit `freshness` metadata on the normalized state: `energy`, `happy`, and relevant `drugCooldown`, `boosterCooldown`, and `inventory` must be `LIVE`; `gym` and `gainModifiers` may be `LIVE` or `FRESH`. `gainPerks` and `activeEffects` must each be explicit arrays, including empty arrays when verified absent. `inventoryFreshness` is accepted for the existing normalized inventory class. Missing or stale material state returns `NEEDS_REFRESH`, while plans can still be modeled and ranked. This is a source-agnostic contract; there is no built-in timestamp or refresh operation.

For a refill plan to show `READY`, normalized `pointRefill.allowed` and `fillAmountPolicy: 'natural_max'` must be verified, `pointsRequired` and `pointsAvailable` must be explicit nonnegative integers with enough points available, and `freshness.pointRefill` and `freshness.points` must both be `LIVE`. The second phase includes `VERIFY_STATE` with expected Energy, stat, and Happy; any differing observation must trigger replanning. Unknown point quantity excludes the refill option, while unknown point value excludes it from economic objectives without inventing a price.

## Limits of this checkpoint

- The 001A model's live calibration applies only to the documented observed domain. Other supported pre-50m arithmetic is identified as extrapolation. No post-50m outcome is forecast.
- Happy gain from items, cooldowns, prices, availability, point values, and future Happy must be supplied by a future authorized adapter or explicit manual normalization. An unknown resource cost is `null`, not zero. Gain-only modes can still compare supported owned-item paths when replacement value is unknown.
- The Happy recipe engine models integer Happy effects and an explicitly supplied Ecstasy multiplier. It retains mixed owned unknown-value and known-value boosters for gain-only ranking with unknown economics. Fractional outcomes and unsupported special effects abstain. No overdose odds, success probability, long-horizon projection, or unsupplied regeneration timing is invented.
- `BALANCED_STATS` allocation is explicitly unsupported because the frozen specification supplies no train-by-train allocation rule. `WEAKEST_STAT` picks the lowest normalized stat with lexical ties; `TARGET_STAT` uses the selected stat.
- Only meaningful Energy checkpoints are generated; a scheduled Happy preparation after a cooldown requires a normalized future Happy value. If Xanax's resulting cooldown is unknown, Xanax→Ecstasy composition abstains. A later Ecstasy wait from below natural Energy cap also abstains until future Energy is observed; no future Energy is inferred for that second wait. Play-window timing and additional gym special mechanics remain outside this slice.
- No user-facing UI or live TornPDA behavior has been exercised. The future adapter and presentation phase requires a separate authorization and manual desktop/TornPDA gate.
